Photo recognition

SnapCal can read your meal photo with Claude and estimate what is on the plate. Paste your own Anthropic API key — it is stored only in this browser and sent only to api.anthropic.com.

A browser-stored key can be read by anything that can run script on this page. Use a key with a low spend limit, and clear it when you are done.

Appearance

Your data

Everything lives in this browser — meals in local storage, photos in an on-device database. Nothing is uploaded except the photo you choose to analyse.

SnapCal gives estimates for everyday awareness, not clinical accuracy. Calorie figures in the built-in food list are rounded reference values, and a photo estimate is a best guess from what is visible.
